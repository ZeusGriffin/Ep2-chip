# NODE Title Sequence

Required boot sequence for BLINK Pocket Desktop.

1. Start on black.
2. `NODE` enters from the top and moves downward in stepped, old-school video-game motion.
3. Visual language: minimal Nothing-style black/white/gray, sparse pixel/dot accents, no clutter.
4. When NODE settles, show a very small `Made by ZUZ` directly underneath.
5. Hold roughly 0.8–1.2 seconds.
6. Cut/fade quickly to the BLINK launcher.
7. Keep the full sequence short and make it skippable once buttons are mapped.

The motion should feel intentionally retro/stepped rather than modern eased animation.
