Pocket Tank: A tiny fish tank where the fish think for themselves.

A real AI lives inside a board you can hold in one hand. It decides when each fish eats, explores, plays or rests. No internet. No app. No cloud.

What it is

A pet you keep on your desk

Pocket Tank is a little aquarium on a little screen. You take care of it, and the fish live their own lives.

Fish with their own minds

Every fish has a personality. Some are bold, some are shy. Every few seconds each one looks around and decides what to do next. When it can't make up its mind, you'll see it hesitate.

A tank you look after

Tap the water to feed them. Wipe algae off the glass. Trim the grass. Hold a finger on the glass and the fish that trust you swim over to say hi.

It keeps living

Put it to sleep and it keeps track of time. Come back tomorrow and the fish are hungry, the grass has grown, and maybe there's a new baby waiting.

Life in the tank

It grows with you

Nothing here is scripted. Everything happens because of how you care for the tank, and it's all saved.

Raise a family

You start with two baby fish, and you name them. Feed them and they grow up. Take good care of them, and a pair will dance in the grass and have a baby of their own.

Every newborn gets its colors from its parents, and a name from you. The tank grows up to six fish.

Get to know them

Tap a fish to see its card: how hungry it is, how tired, how it's feeling, and how much it trusts you.

A new fish's card starts mostly blank. You learn who it is by watching it.

Earn badges

Each fish earns badges for its firsts: first meal from your hand, first trip to the reef, first time it swims over when you call. The tank earns its own for your chores.

A checklist shows what the tank needs before the next baby can arrive, with tips for each step.

Decorate with sand dollars

Taking care of the tank earns sand dollars. Spend them in the shop:

  • A sword plant for more cover
  • A snail that cleans the glass, even at night
  • A castle the fish swim through
  • A coral you color yourself, which grows for weeks
  • A whole reef that blooms with tentacles
How it works

A tiny brain, all on the board

The fish are run by a small language model: the same kind of AI as a chatbot, shrunk way down. A big AI watched tens of thousands of fish situations and said what each fish should do. Then a tiny one learned to copy it, small enough to fit on the chip.

Get one

Get your own Pocket Tank

Set one up yourself

No soldering, no coding. It takes about two minutes once you have a board.

  1. Pick a board. The original is a Waveshare 1.8″ screen for about $30. There is also a round pendant and a watch. Compare them all.
  2. Plug it into your computer with a USB-C cable.
  3. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge and click Install. Two baby fish are waiting when it's done.

Update any time. Your fish stay.

Updates install from the same page with the same button. An update replaces the app and the fish brain. It never touches the part of the board where your tank is saved.

  • Your fish and their names
  • Their trust, growth and families
  • Every badge you've earned
  • Your sand dollars
  • Everything you've bought and placed
  • How clean the glass is (sorry)

Every new version can read tanks saved by every older one, and each update is checked against real saved tanks before it goes out. Going back to an older version keeps your tank too. The only ways to lose a tank are on purpose: the reset (hold BOOT and tap the glass, then answer YES) or the erase button on the install page.
