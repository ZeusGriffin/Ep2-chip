Prices are Waveshare's on October 3, 2026, before shipping. Every board comes with or without a battery: pick the one with a battery if you want to take your tank off the cable. Battery times are for a full charge. Measured means timed on our own tanks; an estimate scales the 1.8's measured sleep to the bigger battery (a switched-off board draws about the same on either), and gets replaced once we've timed it. On screen, the watch uses more power, so its bigger battery doesn't last much longer; the pendant draws about what the 1.8 does, so its 500 mAh battery plays about three times as long. The clock chip is what lets a tank switch itself fully off at night and still know how long you were away. The pendant has no clock chip, so it keeps the board's own clock running while it sleeps, which uses more battery. We're working on that.
