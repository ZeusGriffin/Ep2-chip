Something wrong with your tank?

Start with the quick fixes. If they don't do it, the tank check reads what your tank says is wrong.

The screen is black

Most of the time the tank is just in its deep sleep. Your fish are saved. Try these in order.

  1. Hold PWR for one full second, then let go. After 20 minutes asleep the tank powers all the way down. From there a quick tap does nothing, and tapping the glass does nothing. A firm one-second press starts it.
  2. Unplug the cable and plug it back in. Power arriving starts a sleeping tank by itself. If it was not plugged in, the battery may be empty: plug it in and give it a few minutes.
  3. Force a restart. Hold PWR for a full 10 seconds. Count them. Let go, then hold PWR for one second. This cuts the power no matter what the tank was doing. Your tank saves itself as you go, so you lose a few minutes at most.
  4. Still black? Plug it into a computer and run the tank check below. It tells you, and us, what is going on.

Dim, not black? The tank light is off and the fish are resting. Tap the glass twice, quickly, to turn it on.

Tank check

Plug the tank into your computer with a USB-C cable that carries data, then click the button and pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit. This page listens to the tank for a few seconds and tells you what it found.

This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. You can still paste a log below. Chrome or Edge on a computer · nothing is installed or changed

The report is the findings above plus everything the tank said. It has your fish's names in it. Wi-Fi names are removed. Where to send it.

Restart and check again restarts the tank, the same as unplugging it, and reads its startup. It changes nothing in your tank.

Other problems

My tank is not in the list when I plug it in

A tank in its deep sleep is not on USB. Hold PWR for one full second first, then try again.

Try another cable. Some cables only charge. Plug straight into the computer, not a hub.

Still missing? Hold BOOT while you plug in the cable. That always makes the board show up, but in install mode: the screen stays black and the tank check has nothing to read. From there, reinstall from the install page. It keeps your tank.

The picture is frozen

Force a restart: hold PWR for a full 10 seconds, let go, then hold it for one second. Then please run the tank check and send us the report. A tank should never freeze.

It turns on, then goes dark again

The battery is empty or not connected. Leave it on the cable for an hour. If it only runs while plugged in, check that the battery's plug is seated on the board.

The screen went black after I held BOOT

Holding BOOT while the board gets power puts it in install mode, with a black screen. Unplug the cable, plug it back in without touching any key, and hold PWR for one second.

I woke it and the fish are starving

That's the tank working. Time passes while it sleeps, so a tank left for a day wakes up hungry. Tap the water to feed them.

An update or install went wrong

Install again from the install page with the main button. It never erases your tank. That page has its own fixes for cables, drivers and browsers.

Nothing works. Can I start clean?

Yes. On the install page, Erase the board and install fresh wipes everything and installs again. Your tank will be gone. If you can, run the tank check and send us the report first, so we can find what went wrong.

Ask for help

Stuck, or just curious? Ask in the Pocket Tank Q&A on GitHub. Other keepers read it, and so do we.

It helps a lot if you include:

  • The report from the tank check. Paste it in, or attach the file.
  • What the tank was doing before it went wrong, and whether it was on the cable or on battery.
  • Which board you have.

Found a bug you can repeat? Open an issue instead.
