Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 4, 2026.

The short version

  • We only collect what you give us: your email, if you join the newsletter.
  • We never sell or share it, and there are no ads on this site.
  • Google Analytics only runs if you tap Allow. You can change your mind any time.
  • Your tank runs on its own. It sends nothing about you or your fish anywhere.

Who we are

Pocket Tank and pocketank.com are made by StratoBuilds. Questions, or want something deleted? Email strato@pocketank.com.

The newsletter

If you sign up, we keep:

  • your email address,
  • whether you ticked "I'd like to buy a ready-made Pocket Tank",
  • the date, and which page you signed up from.

We use it only to email you about Pocket Tank, and to get a sense of how many people would buy a ready-made one. The list is stored on our web server, in a place no web page can reach. When someone signs up, a short note with their email goes to our team's private chat, so we know the list is working.

To stop for a moment that someone floods the form, we keep a scrambled code of your connection (not your address itself) for one hour, then throw it away.

Every email has a link to unsubscribe. You can also just email us. If we ever move the list to an email service (like Mailchimp), we'll update this page first.

Analytics

We'd like to know which pages help people and how visitors interact with the tools on this site, so we ask to use Google Analytics. It only runs if you tap Allow on the small card. Until then, nothing loads from Google Analytics and no analytics cookies are set.

If you allow it, Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga) and records things like the pages you visit, how you found us, your rough location (city or country), and your device and browser type. We never turn on advertising features. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we take that as "No thanks" and don't ask.

Choosing "No thanks" later also removes the _ga cookies from this site. Your choice is remembered in your browser only.

Other services this site uses

  • Our web host (SiteGround) keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.
  • YouTube videos only load when you press play. Before that, the picture comes from our own server. They play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com).
  • GitHub hosts the firmware. The install page downloads it from there when you install.

The installer and the help page

Installing, setting up Wi-Fi and the tank check on the help page all happen between your browser and your board, over the USB cable. If you type your Wi-Fi password during setup, it goes straight to your tank. We never see it.

Your tank

Pocket Tank runs entirely on the board. The fish, their brain and your save never leave it.

If you give your tank Wi-Fi, it only goes online to:

  • check GitHub for new versions, and download one when you say so,
  • get the time from public time servers (like time.google.com, time.cloudflare.com and pool.ntp.org).

It doesn't send anything about you, your tank or how you use it. Your Wi-Fi name and password stay on the board.

Your choices

  • Ask what we have about you, or ask us to delete it: email strato@pocketank.com.
  • Leave the newsletter with the link in any email.
  • Change your analytics choice with the button above, or the "Analytics settings" link at the bottom of every page.

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them. If you think a child signed up, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
